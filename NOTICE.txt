EmbeddingGemma
--------------

The bundled embedding model is a quantized ORT conversion of Google's
EmbeddingGemma 300M model. TrainerPro redistributes the pinned converted model
and tokenizer bytes from Moonshine AI's model catalog without further
modification.

Gemma is provided under and subject to the Gemma Terms of Use found at ai.google.dev/gemma/terms

TrainerPro includes a copy of the applicable Gemma agreement, the incorporated
Prohibited Use Policy, and model-specific downstream restrictions in:

- legal/GEMMA_PROHIBITED_USE_POLICY_2024-02-21.txt
- BINARY_DISTRIBUTION_TERMS.md

Use and distribution of the bundled EmbeddingGemma model are subject to the
restrictions in those documents.

The Gemma Prohibited Use Policy is incorporated into those terms:
ai.google.dev/gemma/prohibited_use_policy

Model information:

Moonshine Voice
---------------

TrainerPro uses a modified single-thread WebAssembly build of Moonshine Voice
0.1.5. The runtime modifications provide WKWebView and bundler compatibility;
the bundled English Small Streaming speech model is not modified by
TrainerPro. Moonshine Voice software and the English streaming model are
provided under the MIT License.

Tokenizer artifacts
-------------------

Moonshine's binary-tokenizer runtime is part of Moonshine Voice and uses its
MIT license. The English streaming tokenizer is part of the MIT-licensed
Moonshine model. The EmbeddingGemma tokenizer is part of the EmbeddingGemma

not ship the SentencePiece software library.
